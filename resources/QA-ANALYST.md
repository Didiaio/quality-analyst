# QUALITY ANALYST

## EXCEL

- Excel sheet checks for key metrics
- Power BI - Data Report Review (Export data - xls, csv, etc.)
- Review and Analyze data for department performance and agent performance

## REPORTING EXPECTATION

- Daily - MTD update on Performance Metrics
- Weekly - Report week on week performance and trends
- Monthly - Report month on month performance and trends, identify areas of improvement and recommend actions
- Quarterly - Comprehensive performance review, identify long-term trends, and strategic recommendations for improvement

## OPERATION CHAIN

- Real-time monitoring of performance metrics
- Regular audits of data accuracy and integrity
- Feedback loop with agents and supervisors for continuous improvement
- Collaboration with other departments to ensure alignment on performance goals and metrics
- Implementation of corrective actions based on performance data analysis

## REPORT CHAIN

- Quality Manager - Review and approve reports before distribution
- Senior Management - Receive high-level reports for strategic decision-making
- Operations Manager - Receive reports for operational decision-making
- Team Leaders - Use reports to guide team performance and coaching
- Agents - Receive feedback based on report findings for performance improvement

NB: CC relevant stakeholders in report distribution to ensure transparency and accountability.

## QA ANALYST RESPONSIBILITIES

- Monitor and analyze performance metrics to identify trends and areas for improvement
- Conduct regular audits of data accuracy and integrity
- Provide feedback to agents and supervisors for continuous improvement
- Collaborate with other departments to ensure alignment on performance goals and metrics

## REPORT OUTLINE

- Date
- Agent
- Team Leader
- Interaction ID
- Performance Metrics (e.g., AHT, FCR, CSAT, etc.)
- Category of Performance
- Comments/Recommendations for Improvement
- Action Taken (if applicable)

## QA FORMULA

= CATEGORY + COUNT = PERCENTAGE

Where:

- CATEGORY: The category of performance
- COUNT: The number of instances within that category
- PERCENTAGE: The calculated percentage representing the proportion of instances in that category relative to the total count
